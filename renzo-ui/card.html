what we do

Renzo is a local-first studio that turns your words into working things — small tools, scripts, apps, pages. You describe what you want in plain language. Renzo plans, builds, and quietly puts the result on your desktop.

There are no settings to fiddle with, no models to choose. That is the whole point. The studio knows what to use; you describe the work.

Everything lives on your machine. Nothing phones home. What Renzo makes is yours — open it, change it, throw it away. No accounts. No lock-in.

Named for Renzo Piano, who builds buildings that show their structure. We tried to build software the same way.
