II
Manifesto

A small letter
about making things,
quietly.

Written by the studio · Spring, 2026
i.

On making things.

The world does not need more software. It needs fewer, better things — made with care, by people who cared to make them.

Renzo exists because most software is built like a sandwich. Layers of abstraction, applied by people who don't know who's eating it. The result is fine. The result is always fine. Nothing is bad. Nothing is good. Nothing has a maker.

We are not interested in this. We are interested in the small things — a tool that organises your photos, a script that renames your files, a page that lives on a friend's computer. Things you could have made yourself, if you had a week and the right cup of coffee. Renzo is that week, and that cup of coffee.

ii.

On attention.

The most expensive thing in the world is your afternoon. Every notification, every dashboard, every "AI is thinking…" spinner costs you a few seconds of focus you will not get back.

Renzo takes your attention seriously. It does not interrupt. It does not chat. It does not send you messages. It finishes, and then it tells you, once.

This is the only honest relationship a tool can have with a person today. Make the work. Step back. Tell them when it's done. Let them come back to it on their own time.

iii.

On the studio.

Renzo runs on your machine. It does not phone home. It does not train on your work. It does not, in any meaningful sense, exist outside of your computer.

We chose this on purpose. Local software is more honest than cloud software, because it respects the old bargain: you own the thing you paid for.

There is also a quieter reason. The things Renzo makes are personal — a script that organises your photos, a tool that handles your downloads. They belong to you in a way that nothing on a server can. Putting them in the cloud would change what they are.

A studio that listens, makes, and leaves.
Then it's your turn again.

iv.

On being small.

Renzo will not become a platform. There will not be a marketplace. There will not be plugins, integrations, or an "ecosystem." There will be Renzo, and the things Renzo makes for you, and that will be enough.

We are choosing to be small the way a tailor chooses to be small. One client, one garment, one fitting at a time. It is the only way the work stays honest.

If you want what Renzo makes, you can use it forever, change it, throw it away. You don't owe us anything. We don't owe you anything. We just made the thing.

v.

On being named.

Renzo is named after Renzo Piano, who builds buildings that show their structure. You can see the beams. You can see where the light enters. Nothing is hidden.

We tried to build software the same way. The interface shows you what it is doing. The work is visible. The process is not hidden behind a chatbot. When Renzo is working, you can see it. When it has finished, you can see what it made. Nothing in between is a mystery.

Working quietly. Telling you, once, when it is done.

Made with care,

Renzo

The studio · 2026

That's enough talking.

Let's see what we can make.
