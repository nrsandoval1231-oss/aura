IV
First visit

Hello.
What shall we
make today?

The studio is quiet this morning. Tell me anything — a tool, a page, a small thing you've been meaning to make.

i.

Say what you want.

Plain language. No formats, no fields, no technical words. Imagine you're describing it to a friend.

ii.

Wait a few minutes.

Renzo reads what you wrote, plans the work, builds it, checks it, and quietly puts the result on your desktop.

iii.

Take it or leave it.

The work is yours. Open it, change it, throw it away. If it's not what you wanted, say so and Renzo will try again.

Ready when you are.
