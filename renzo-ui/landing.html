Three small promises

What it is, simply.

No dashboards. No jargon. No cloud. Just three things you can count on.

i.
Local
Everything lives on your machine. Your files, your keys, your work. Nothing travels.
ii.
Quiet
No notifications, no chat windows, no model pickers. Renzo finishes, then tells you.
iii.
Yours
What he makes is yours. Open it, change it, throw it away. No accounts. No lock-in.

A studio that listens, makes, and leaves.
Then it's your turn again.
