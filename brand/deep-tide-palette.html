The rules.

05 / how to use
01
The teal is the canvas.

Never compete with it. The accent exists to draw the eye to where action happens, not to add color.

02
One accent, used well.

Warm honey gold is the only bold color. Use it for primary actions, prompts, and the one thing per screen that matters.

03
Status colors stay quiet.

No neon greens or reds. Status colors are desaturated to fit the palette — visible but never loud.
