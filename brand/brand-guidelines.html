/ 04 · voice & tone

What we say, and how we say it.

✓ Do
"Canary is the new production."
Confident, calm, present tense. Treats the user like they already understand.
✗ Don't
"🚀 Wow, your deploy was super fast and successful!"
No emoji. No exclamation. No celebrating the obvious. The work speaks for itself.
✓ Do
"Type faster. Ship calmer."
Short. Verbs. Benefits, not features. Two-line rhythm.
✗ Don't
"The most powerful, AI-native, next-generation CLI platform."
No superlatives. No buzzwords. We earn trust by being precise, not loud.
