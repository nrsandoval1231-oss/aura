# Aura learning acceptance

A live improvement claim requires a recorded baseline, attributable reviewed lesson,
retrieval by a later compatible slice, a recorded change in strategy, and a measured
outcome under compatible required checks. Preserve negative knowledge and withdraw harm.
Compare quality before speed, repair count, cost, or interruptions. Missing or incompatible
evidence remains UNKNOWN. Independent review verifies attribution and comparability.

The deterministic proofs in tests/fixtures/proofs exercise the causal mechanism and
rollback. They do not certify live model improvement, general policy promotion, model
weight changes, or statistical significance across unrelated slices.
