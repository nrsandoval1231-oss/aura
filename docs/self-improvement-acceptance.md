# Governed self-improvement acceptance contract

This contract is a mandatory V0 completion gate. It makes self-improvement an
observable engineering capability rather than passive logging or prompt storage.

## Canonical trace

This contract implements the intent of Canonical Architecture Sections 216–222,
233, 238, 263, and 268:

`STATE → DECISION → ACTION → OUTCOME → DETERMINISTIC EVALUATION → ATTRIBUTION → CANDIDATE LESSON → VALIDATE OR REJECT → PROMOTE WITH SCOPE → APPLY TO FUTURE WORK → MEASURE IMPROVEMENT → RETAIN, REVISE, DEMOTE, OR ROLLBACK`

## Required evidence

Every future learning episode must retain:

1. attributable experience and outcome records, including the exact repository,
   candidate, packet, strategy, evidence, and named directional metric;
2. a candidate lesson with context, diagnosis, intended behavior change, and
   negative knowledge where an approach failed;
3. an independent validation or rejection decision with evidence and scope;
4. repository-scoped promotion in V0; automatic global promotion is disabled;
5. retrieval evidence showing the lesson was available during later planning;
6. a later decision receipt proving whether the retrieved lesson changed the
   selected strategy or packet;
7. a post-application measurement against the named outcome, with UNKNOWN when
   attribution or evidence is insufficient;
8. demotion or rollback evidence when the lesson harms the named outcome or
   conflicts with canonical authority;
9. a governed path for eventually modifying Forge's non-protected
   implementation, while never autonomously modifying authority, audit
   independence, security, event integrity, Finish Contract protections, North
   Star ownership, or other protected policy.

## V0 acceptance

The later Learning Engine cannot be accepted merely because it stores lessons.
V0 must preserve the schemas, provenance, candidate identity, outcome linkage,
metric baseline, scope, and decision receipts needed to prove that a validated
lesson changed later engineering behavior and improved a named outcome. The
Finish Contract therefore remains unsatisfied until a later phase demonstrates
that causal chain on a subsequent bounded build.
