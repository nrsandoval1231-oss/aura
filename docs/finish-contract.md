# Aura finish contract

Aura is complete only when the exact integrated candidate has independent review and
real evidence for the owner's architecture: one governed task, two independent builder
loops, dependency/conflict-aware integration, enforced stuck recovery, interruption/restart
reconciliation, trusted lesson review, and attributable improvement on a later task.

Scope, authority, budgets, candidate binding, context independence, and UNKNOWN handling
must hold throughout. Required checks may not be weakened by learning. Harmful lessons
must be withdrawn while history remains intact. Provider roles and production signing
trust must be verified. Offline proofs and a green source validation gate are necessary
mechanism checks, not evidence that these live acceptance conditions have been met.
